Lore files

location

Vice City, 1986

The 2002 classic turned the series into a period piece: pastel suits, synth-pop, oceanfront clubs, and a criminal economy with a taste for chrome. Tommy Vercetti arrives in a city that rewards confidence and punishes sentiment, and the game's radio dial becomes as memorable as its skyline.

Wikipedia · GTA Wiki

character

Tommy Vercetti

A made man with a long memory and a short temper. Tommy arrives in Vice City expecting a debt to be settled and decides to collect the whole city instead. His story is about refusing to be anyone's subordinate.

Wikipedia · GTA Wiki

faction

The Vercetti Organisation

A crew built out of borrowed loyalty and purchased property. By the end of 1986 it controls clubs, businesses, and the kind of silence that money can buy.

Wikipedia · GTA Wiki

character

Ricardo Diaz

A wealthy Vice City drug baron whose mansion, connections, and paranoia make him both a useful patron and an obvious target. Diaz represents the older criminal order that Tommy Vercetti learns to read, then replace.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Ricardo Diaz" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.

character

Lance Vance

A charming, resentful partner whose family history ties him to Vice City's drug trade. Lance works best as a mirror for Tommy: both want a way in, but only one is willing to wait for it.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Lance Vance" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.

location

The Malibu Club

A neon nightclub on Vice City's waterfront that doubles as a front for planning bigger jobs. Buying the Malibu is one of the story's clearest status shifts: from hired muscle to someone with a venue, a crew, and a reason to be feared.

Story beats

1986 Tommy Vercetti gets out

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

A prison sentence ends and a new one begins: a drug deal on the beach goes wrong, and Tommy has to improvise.

Tommy arrives in Vice City expecting a clean handoff. The deal is ambushed, the money disappears, and the people who sent him want answers. The game's opening is a masterclass in tone: sun, neon, and immediate violence.

1986 The Vercetti rise

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

Tommy works his way through the city's power brokers, buying property and collecting enemies in equal measure.

The middle act is an empire-building montage: clubs, businesses, assassination contracts, and a growing crew. Tommy's refusal to be a subordinate turns a debt into a takeover, and Vice City starts to feel like his city.

1986 Lance Vance changes sides

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

The alliance at the centre of Tommy's rise starts to fracture. Loyalty in Vice City is always a temporary arrangement.

Lance's resentment and Sonny Forelli's pressure converge. Tommy's empire is strong enough to survive the city, but not strong enough to survive the people closest to it. The betrayal sets up the final confrontation.

1986 The last stand at the mansion

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

Sonny comes to collect, Lance makes his choice, and Tommy settles the debt with a very loud house call.

The finale is a siege: Tommy alone against Sonny's men, with Lance on the wrong side. When it ends, Tommy is the last one standing in a mansion that now belongs to him. The city is quiet for exactly one sunset.

1984 Victor Vance arrives

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories

A soldier returns to Vice City looking for a clean way out and finds a city that only trades in complications.

Victor Vance's story is a tragedy in slow motion: family loyalty, military connections, and a drug economy that turns every favour into a debt. The game ends with him alive but compromised, which makes the opening of Vice City land harder.

1984 The Vance empire

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories

Victor builds a criminal operation out of borrowed muscle and family obligation. Every new asset comes with a new enemy.

By the end of Vice City Stories, Victor controls territory, businesses, and a growing reputation. The cost is the relationship with his brother Lance and the knowledge that the city always collects. His death in the opening of Vice City is the punchline to this setup.
