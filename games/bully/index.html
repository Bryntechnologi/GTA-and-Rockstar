Lore files

location

Bullworth Academy, 2006

Bully trades city blocks for school corridors, cliques, and small-town gossip. Jimmy Hopkins navigates a private academy where the hierarchy is absurdly serious and the calendar is a social weapon. It is a Rockstar game about territory at a much smaller scale, with the same eye for satire.

Wikipedia · GTA Wiki

faction

Bullworth's cliques

Bullworth Academy is a small world with big politics: jocks, preppies, greasers, nerds, and everyone else trying to survive the timetable. The satire is sharp because the stakes feel real.

Wikipedia · GTA Wiki

character

Jimmy Hopkins

A teenager dropped into Bullworth Academy and forced to learn its hierarchy faster than he can make friends. Jimmy is a Rockstar protagonist in miniature: stubborn, resourceful, and happier solving a problem with a plan than with an apology.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Jimmy Hopkins" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.

GTA Wiki

character

Gary Smith

Bullworth's most manipulative student, who treats every clique as a lever and every friendship as a temporary tool. Gary turns the school's social map into a strategy game, then dares everyone else to keep up.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Gary Smith" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.
