Bullworth Academy, 2006

Bully trades city blocks for school corridors, cliques, and small-town gossip. Jimmy Hopkins navigates a private academy where the hierarchy is absurdly serious and the calendar is a social weapon. It is a Rockstar game about territory at a much smaller scale, with the same eye for satire.

Bullworth's cliques

Bullworth Academy is a small world with big politics: jocks, preppies, greasers, nerds, and everyone else trying to survive the timetable. The satire is sharp because the stakes feel real.
