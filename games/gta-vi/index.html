The revealed premise follows Jason and Lucia through a version of Vice City and the wider state of Leonida. The tone is modern, sunlit, and tense: a relationship story wrapped around a crime spree. Specific plot details are intentionally left out here rather than invented.

2026 The state of Leonida

Grand Theft Auto VI

Causeways, wetlands, small towns, and a neon capital. The map is a state, not just a city.

Leonida expands the Vice City idea into a whole peninsula: Port Gellhorn's docks, Grassrivers' wetlands, Mount Kalaga's ridges, and the Keys' island roads. It is the broadest Rockstar map yet, and the setting is the story's co-star.

2026 The next score

Grand Theft Auto VI

A partnership, a state full of opportunities, and the oldest question in the series: how much is enough?

The central tension of the revealed premise is trust: two people trying to build something together in a place designed to separate them. Everything else — the cars, the clubs, the coastline — is the scenery around that question.

2026 A world that keeps moving

The series' next era is built around a living state: traffic, weather, wildlife, and a news cycle that never quite looks away.

Rockstar's pitch for GTA VI is density: a world with more systems, more interiors, and more ways for a plan to go sideways. The story is only one thread in a state that keeps generating its own.
