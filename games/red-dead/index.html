location

The Frontier, 1899

Red Dead Redemption 2 is a prequel about the end of an era: a gang trying to stay useful while the world builds fences and railroads around it. Snow, swamp, prairie, and city all share the same slow rhythm. The frontier here is less a place to conquer than a story people keep telling themselves.

Wikipedia · GTA Wiki

character

Arthur Morgan

A career outlaw and the emotional centre of Red Dead Redemption 2, caught between loyalty to his gang and the evidence that the world is closing around it. Arthur's story is less about redemption as a prize than about choosing what kind of person to be before the end.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Arthur Morgan" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.

GTA Wiki

character

Dutch van der Linde

The charismatic leader whose vision of freedom keeps a gang together long after the plan has stopped working. Dutch is the frontier's answer to a GTA kingpin: a man whose confidence is both the reason people follow and the reason they cannot leave.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Dutch van der Linde" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.

GTA Wiki

location

Saint Denis

Red Dead's New Orleans analogue: a humid, industrial city of streetcars, press, and old money. It is the point where the frontier stops feeling like an escape and starts looking like a system with paperwork.

Adapted from GTA Wiki, "Saint Denis" · CC BY-SA 3.0 · changes may have been made.
