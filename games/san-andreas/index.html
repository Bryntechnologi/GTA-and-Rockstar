Lore files

location

San Andreas, 1992

Three cities, one enormous state, and a homecoming story about family, loyalty, and territory. San Andreas mixes early-90s West Coast culture with a road-trip structure: Los Santos, San Fierro, and Las Venturas each bring a different rhythm, skyline, and kind of trouble.

Source

character

Carl 'CJ' Johnson

A returning son who spends most of San Andreas trying to keep his family together while the city pulls it apart. CJ is loyal, practical, and increasingly aware that loyalty is not a plan.

Source

faction

Grove Street Families

A neighbourhood outfit defined by history, loyalty, and a losing war for territory. Grove Street is less a gang than a memory of one, which is exactly what makes it worth fighting for.

Source

Story beats

1992 CJ comes home

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Five years away, a funeral, and a neighbourhood that has moved on without him. Carl Johnson returns to Los Santos.

The opening of San Andreas is a homecoming story: CJ finds his mother dead, his family scattered, and the Grove Street Families losing ground. The game's first act is about rebuilding trust before it becomes about rebuilding an empire.

1992 Grove Street vs the Ballas

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Territory, drive-bys, and a family under pressure. The neighbourhood war escalates while the people closest to CJ make other plans.

The gang war is both personal and structural: the Ballas have backing, Grove has history, and the police treat the whole block as a problem to manage. CJ's loyalty is tested long before the bigger conspiracy surfaces.

1992 San Fierro and the Loco Syndicate

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

The story widens beyond Los Santos: a new city, a new garage, and a drug network with ties back home.

San Fierro gives CJ a base, a cover business, and a target. The Loco Syndicate arc connects the street-level betrayal to a larger criminal supply chain, and introduces Mike Toreno as a very different kind of antagonist.

1992 The Caligula's heist

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Las Venturas, a casino, and a plan that depends on too many people behaving predictably.

The Caligula's arc is San Andreas at its most cinematic: surveillance, preparation, and a heist that becomes a power struggle between criminal organisations. It is also where the game's three-city structure pays off.

1992 The homecoming

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Los Santos riots, old friends return, and CJ finally gets to settle the score with the people who sold out Grove Street.

The final act ties the gang war, the police corruption, and the family betrayal together. CJ's return to Grove Street is not a victory lap; it is a cleanup. The last confrontation is personal, and the city keeps burning behind it.
