Lore files

location

Liberty City, 2008

Grand Theft Auto IV trades sunshine for overcast steel. Niko Bellic arrives in Liberty City chasing an idea of success and finds a colder city of immigrants, debts, and old wars. The game's tone is grounded, its streets dense, and its story less about empire than about the cost of trying to leave one behind.

Story beats

2008 Niko Bellic arrives

Grand Theft Auto IV

A war veteran comes to Liberty City chasing an old promise and finds his cousin's life was mostly a sales pitch.

Niko's arrival is the series' most grounded opening: a cramped apartment, a cousin with debts, and a city that offers work before it offers answers. The immigrant story gives GTA IV its emotional weight.

2008 Blood and debts

Grand Theft Auto IV

Niko's search for the man who betrayed his unit pulls him into loan sharks, gangsters, and a war he did not start.

The middle of GTA IV is a web of obligation: Roman's gambling, Dimitri's manipulations, and the gradual revelation of what happened to Niko's squad. The city's optimism curdles into something colder.

2008 Deal or revenge

Grand Theft Auto IV

The story's final choice is not about money or loyalty. It is about whether Niko can live with either answer.

The Deal ending costs Roman his life; the Revenge ending costs Kate hers. GTA IV refuses a clean victory. Whichever path the player chooses, Liberty City keeps its ledger balanced and Niko keeps carrying the cost.

2008 The diamond deal

Grand Theft Auto IV

A bag of diamonds passes through too many hands, linking Niko's story to the two episodes that follow.

The diamond exchange is the connective tissue of the GTA IV era: a single botched deal that pulls in the Lost MC, Gay Tony's nightlife, and Niko's own circle. It is the series' clearest example of a shared criminal ecosystem.

2009 The Lost MC fractures

Grand Theft Auto IV: The Lost and Damned

Johnny Klebitz tries to hold a motorcycle club together while its president pushes it toward a war it cannot win.

The Lost and Damned is a story about loyalty in a culture that mistakes loyalty for obedience. Johnny's attempts to keep the club solvent collide with Billy Grey's recklessness, and the fallout leaves the Lost weaker and angrier.

2009 Nightlife on the edge

Grand Theft Auto IV: The Ballad of Gay Tony

Luis Lopez keeps Tony Prince's clubs running while the money, the drugs, and the Russians all want a piece.

The Ballad of Gay Tony is the flashiest of the GTA IV stories and the most anxious underneath. Luis is the competent one in a room full of people who have already spent the money. The finale ties back to the diamond deal and closes the IV-era trilogy.
