FIVE STAR WANTED // ARCADE

Play free GTA trivia and Rockstar puzzle games.

FIVE STAR WANTED is a fan-made Rockstar lore archive with a free browser arcade. Test your GTA trivia knowledge across Vice City, San Andreas, Liberty City, Los Santos, and Leonida, or switch to Rockstar puzzle games and crack a vault, plot a patrol route, or deduce a district from field notes. No account and no download.

Play the GTA trivia quiz Play the puzzle games

Two ways to play

Quiz

GTA Trivia Quiz

A free GTA trivia quiz written from public franchise knowledge, with a source note after each answer. Filter by era or play the daily run that refreshes at 00:00 UTC.

  • Era filters: Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV, GTA V, GTA VI, and classic GTA.
  • Multiple formats, including fast runs, streak runs, and Five Star Wanted mode.
  • Keyboard-friendly, mobile-friendly, and replayable with local best scores.
Start a trivia run

Puzzles

Rockstar Puzzle Games

Three generated puzzle loops built for the theme. Every board is replayable, and daily variants use the same UTC seed for everyone.

  • Vault Cracker — deduce a safe code from feedback clues.
  • Heat Grid — plot a route through patrols without getting caught.
  • Clue Dossier — identify a district from field notes and map hints.
Open the puzzles

Daily trivia and daily puzzles

Both daily challenges use the UTC date as their seed, so everyone gets the same run for that day. A local streak tracks how many days in a row you have played. Progress, badges, and preferences are stored in your browser with localStorage — there is no sign-up and no server-side profile.

The arcade is part of a non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

How to play

  1. Open the GTA trivia quiz or the Rockstar puzzle games.
  2. Pick an era or a daily challenge, then choose a format or puzzle type.
  3. Play in the browser. Scores, streaks, and badges save locally on your device.
  4. Want the lore behind an answer? Follow the source note or browse the full-text lore archive.

Before or after you play
