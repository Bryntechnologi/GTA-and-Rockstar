Leonida & Vice City

GTA VI / Leonida · location

Grand Theft Auto VI returns to the fictional state of Leonida, a sun-bleached peninsula of causeways, wetlands, and neon coastline. Vice City is its loudest address: a modern resort metropolis built for money, music, and bad decisions. The revealed story follows Jason and Lucia through a state that treats every sunset like a premiere.

Leonida · Vice City · Jason & Lucia

Vice City, 1986

Vice City · location

The 2002 classic turned the series into a period piece: pastel suits, synth-pop, oceanfront clubs, and a criminal economy with a taste for chrome. Tommy Vercetti arrives in a city that rewards confidence and punishes sentiment, and the game's radio dial becomes as memorable as its skyline.

1986 · Tommy Vercetti · synth

San Andreas, 1992

San Andreas · location

Three cities, one enormous state, and a homecoming story about family, loyalty, and territory. San Andreas mixes early-90s West Coast culture with a road-trip structure: Los Santos, San Fierro, and Las Venturas each bring a different rhythm, skyline, and kind of trouble.

1992 · Los Santos · CJ

Los Santos, 2013

GTA V / Los Santos · location

Grand Theft Auto V splits its story across three protagonists and one sprawling satirical metropolis. Michael, Franklin, and Trevor move between heists, hobbies, and the entertainment industry's endless appetite for reinvention. The result is a West Coast playground built around planning, improvisation, and spectacle.

2013 · three protagonists · heists

Liberty City, 2008

GTA IV / Liberty City · location

Grand Theft Auto IV trades sunshine for overcast steel. Niko Bellic arrives in Liberty City chasing an idea of success and finds a colder city of immigrants, debts, and old wars. The game's tone is grounded, its streets dense, and its story less about empire than about the cost of trying to leave one behind.

2008 · Niko Bellic · Liberty City

The Warriors, 1979

The Warriors · location

Rockstar's brawler expands Walter Hill's cult film into a long, dangerous night across a stylized New York. Gangs own the subway map, turf is measured in blocks and platforms, and the journey home becomes the whole story. The game's look leans on graffiti, turnstiles, and fluorescent light.

Red Dead Redemption 2 is a prequel about the end of an era: a gang trying to stay useful while the world builds fences and railroads around it. Snow, swamp, prairie, and city all share the same slow rhythm. The frontier here is less a place to conquer than a story people keep telling themselves.

The franchise, from 1997

All games · franchise

Grand Theft Auto began as a top-down crime game from DMA Design and grew into one of the most recognizable open-world series in the medium. Each era added a new city, a new voice, and a new way to measure freedom: from arcade chaos to cinematic scale.

1997 · history · DMA Design

Rockstar Games

All games · franchise

Rockstar Games was established as a publishing label under Take-Two Interactive in 1998. Its studios have built a catalog across crime, westerns, noir, and schoolyard satire, with a house style that favors dense worlds, strong radio soundtracks, and sharply observed fiction.

GTA story timeline

1961 London, before the storm

Grand Theft Auto: London 1961 · Vice City

A top-down crime spree in a city still rebuilding itself. Small crews, borrowed cars, and a lot of bad decisions.

The London expansion strips the series back to period cars, tight streets, and a pre-Beatles underworld. It is less a franchise milestone than a proof of concept: the same chase loop, transplanted into a colder city.

The London protagonist · London

Related: Grand Theft Auto: Vice City dossier · Vice City, 1986 · Tommy Vercetti · The Vercetti Organisation

1969 Swinging London, wrong side of the river

Grand Theft Auto: London 1969 · Vice City

The expansion moves the clock forward and the attitude up. British cars, British gangs, and a city that never quite looks away.

London 1969 leans into the visual language of the era — minis, mods, and a soundtrack of period pastiche. It remains one of the series' strangest side streets: a full GTA game built as an expansion.

The London protagonist · London

Related: Grand Theft Auto: Vice City dossier · London, 1969 · Vice City, 1986 · Tommy Vercetti

1984 Victor Vance arrives

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories · Vice City

A soldier returns to Vice City looking for a clean way out and finds a city that only trades in complications.

Victor Vance's story is a tragedy in slow motion: family loyalty, military connections, and a drug economy that turns every favour into a debt. The game ends with him alive but compromised, which makes the opening of Vice City land harder.

Victor Vance · Lance Vance · Vice City

Related: Grand Theft Auto: Vice City dossier · Lance Vance · Vice City, 1986 · Ricardo Diaz

1984 The Vance empire

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories · Vice City

Victor builds a criminal operation out of borrowed muscle and family obligation. Every new asset comes with a new enemy.

By the end of Vice City Stories, Victor controls territory, businesses, and a growing reputation. The cost is the relationship with his brother Lance and the knowledge that the city always collects. His death in the opening of Vice City is the punchline to this setup.

Victor Vance · Lance Vance · Vice City

1986 Tommy Vercetti gets out

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City · Vice City

A prison sentence ends and a new one begins: a drug deal on the beach goes wrong, and Tommy has to improvise.

Tommy arrives in Vice City expecting a clean handoff. The deal is ambushed, the money disappears, and the people who sent him want answers. The game's opening is a masterclass in tone: sun, neon, and immediate violence.

The middle act is an empire-building montage: clubs, businesses, assassination contracts, and a growing crew. Tommy's refusal to be a subordinate turns a debt into a takeover, and Vice City starts to feel like his city.

1997 Liberty City, top-down

The original crime spree begins: steal cars, take jobs, and climb a ladder made of other people's mistakes.

The first GTA established the template: a nameless criminal, a city grid, and a radio dial. Liberty City is the starting point, but the game quickly moves through San Andreas and Vice City, treating each as a new playground.

1997 Three cities, one getaway

Grand Theft Auto · GTA VI / Leonida

The original game's campaign spans multiple cities and ends in the kind of chase that defined the series.

GTA's structure was already episodic: jobs, betrayals, and a final run for freedom. The details are sparse by modern standards, but the rhythm — plan, drive, improvise — is recognisably the series' DNA.

A retro-futurist city, three gangs, and a protagonist with no name and no patience.

GTA2 leaned into dystopia: corporations, gangs, and a city that feels permanently on the edge of collapse. The game's reputation system and gang loyalty mechanics were early attempts at the living-world design the series would later refine.

The opening of GTA III is still one of the series' cleanest setups: Catalina shoots Claude, leaves him for the police, and accidentally creates a free agent. The rest of the game is Claude climbing back through Liberty City's criminal hierarchy.
